# their load floor and gate_measurements.py imports the validated result. A guessed pin is not validation.
# Reserved columns keep diagnostic fixture grammar compatible; production rows must leave them empty.

# SMOKE JUSTIFICATION, EVERY CELL:
# h15/h31: all-on111 GETp32, separately for fused/split shipped posture.
# h07/h23: read-local off011, its marginal effect with overlap/reorder still on.
# h11/h27: overlap off101, its marginal effect with read-local/reorder still on.
# h01/h17: all-off000 baselines, each execution model; this also carries reorder-off throughput.
# t01/t02 and t03/t04: reorder off/on in each mode, p8 mixed service costs, scored by
# short GET p99.9 with a separate BITCOUNT p99.9 starvation guard. rl0 prevents GET
# bypassing the owner scheduler; overlap1 exercises the actual shipped batch paths.
# h48: fused all-on SETp1, covers write traffic and round-trip latency.
# Multi-key smoke IDs are identified beside their rows: split all-on MGETp8 and fused
# all-on MSETp8 restore BOTH multi-key paths at their measured congestion knee.
#
# SMOKE GIVES UP: the other four knob combinations per mode and most knob/workload

# OFAT cannot prove interactions. No claim of full cross-product coverage belongs to smoke.
